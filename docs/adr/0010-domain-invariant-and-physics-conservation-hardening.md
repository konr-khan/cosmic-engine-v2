# ADR 0010: Domain Invariant & Physics Conservation Hardening

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Following the stabilization of prograde kinematics and the unification of the 3D Astronomical Scene Graph (ADR-0007, ADR-0008, ADR-0009), a comprehensive audit of the core mathematical utilities (`src/utils/cosmicMath/**`) and consumer hooks (`src/hooks/**`) identified five classes of latent mathematical failure modes, numerical singularities, and unverified physical assumptions:

1. **Scattered Magic Numbers & Physical Constant Drift**:
   - Fundamental IAU, WGS-84, and Meeus physical constants were duplicated across modules with minor rounding divergences (e.g. Earth obliquity written as `23.439` vs `23.439281`, Earth radius as `6378.137` vs `6371.0`, and lunar orbital inclination alternately written as `5.14` and `5.145`).
   - Unparameterized non-deterministic `new Date()` calls existed inside pure mathematical algorithms such as `findUpcomingEclipses`.

2. **Chirality Inversions & Falsy Zero Traps**:
   - In `scene/cameras.ts`, Earth's heliocentric longitude was evaluated using loose truthiness (`if (lon)`). At the March Equinox ($\lambda_\oplus = 0^\circ$), `0` evaluated to `false`, falling back to Sun longitude arithmetic and inverting the camera's seasonal axial tilt sign by $46.9^\circ$.
   - Telemetry properties throughout `scene/generator.ts` and `lunar.ts` used `||` default fallbacks, which coerced legitimate $0.0$ values (such as $0.0^\circ$ ecliptic latitude, $0.0$ equation of time, or $0.0$ node proximity) to fallback values.

3. **Angle Remainder Discontinuity & High-Latitude Polar Singularities**:
   - In JavaScript, `%` is the remainder operator rather than the true modulo operator (`-10 % 360 === -10`). Negative angles generated by differences (e.g. $L - \alpha$, $\text{gmst} + \text{lon}$) yielded negative angles rather than canonical $[0^\circ, 360^\circ)$ azimuths, causing path flipping and negative array lookups in historical Chaldean planetary hour rulers.
   - In `calculateLunarEvents`, solving $\cos H_0 = (\sin h_0 - \sin\phi\sin\delta)/(\cos\phi\cos\delta)$ divided by $\cos\phi$. At the exact geographic poles ($\phi = \pm 90^\circ$), $\cos\phi \to 0$, producing division by zero and `NaN`.

4. **Floating-Point Domain Overshoot & Geometric Degeneracy**:
   - In floating-point arithmetic, ratios approaching unity can drift to values such as $1.0000000000000002$. In JavaScript, `Math.asin(1.0000000000000002)` produces `NaN`, causing catastrophic failure in eclipse obscuration, lunar angular radius, and axial projections.
   - Shadow cone apex distance equations ($L = R / \sin\theta$) and Moon distance ratios ($1 - d / L$) divided directly by angular differences without non-zero denominator floors.
   - Exaggerated Keplerian orbital semi-minor axis calculations ($b = a\sqrt{1 - e^2}$) evaluated to `NaN` if $e \ge 1.0$.

5. **Absence of Independent Physics Conservation Law Verification**:
   - Existing unit tests validated outputs against hardcoded expected values produced by the functions themselves, but lacked independent physical verification of fundamental orbital conservation laws (Vis-Viva energy conservation, Keplerian apsidal momentum, and vector antipodality).

---

## Decisions

### 1. SSoT Physical Constants & Parameterized Epochs
Centralized all astronomical constants in [`astroConstants.ts`](../../src/utils/cosmicMath/astroConstants.ts) and removed magic numbers across all domain modules:
* `EARTH_RADIUS_MEAN_KM = 6371.0`
* `MOON_MEAN_DISTANCE_KM = 384400.0`
* Standardized `MOON_ORBIT_INCLINATION_DEG = asDegrees(5.145)` across all 2D and 3D subsystems.
* Parameterized pure functions: `findUpcomingEclipses` defaults its `startDate` to `julianDateToDate(J2000_JD)` for 100% deterministic testability.

### 2. Explicit Non-Falsy Truthiness & Modulo Wrapping
Replaced all loose truthiness checks on continuous angular coordinates with explicit `!== undefined` guards and positive modulo wrapping:
* In `scene/cameras.ts`, protected equinoctial $0^\circ$ heliocentric longitude:
  ```typescript
  const lon = scene.earth.heliocentricLongitude !== undefined
    ? Number(scene.earth.heliocentricLongitude)
    : (((sunLambdaDeg + 180) % 360 + 360) % 360);
  ```
* Replaced loose `||` fallbacks with `??` nullish coalescing across all telemetry and body coordinate records.

### 3. Canonical Positive Modulo Formula & Polar Singularity Guards
* Standardized all cyclic angular arithmetic to the canonical positive modulo formula:
  $$\theta_{\text{canonical}} = (((angle \pmod{360}) + 360) \pmod{360})$$
  Applied across GMST, LST, hour angles, ecliptic longitudes, node longitudes, and Chaldean day-of-week indexing.
* Implemented epsilon denominator floors ($|\text{denom}| < 10^{-9}$) in `calculateLunarEvents` and `refineLunarEventHour`, safely routing polar conditions to circumpolar up/down states with zero `NaN`.

### 4. Mathematical Clamping & Safe Distance Floors
* Clamped every `Math.asin()` argument to $[-1, 1]$:
  ```typescript
  Math.asin(clamp(ratio, -1, 1))
  ```
* Introduced safe distance floors `safeDistanceKm = Math.max(1e-6, distanceKm)` and protected shadow cone apex lengths with `Math.max(1e-6, sinUmbra)`.
* Guarded Keplerian semi-minor axis square roots against negative radicands: `Math.sqrt(Math.max(0, 1 - e * e))`.

### 5. Independent Physics Conservation Law Test Harness
Created a dedicated test suite [`src/utils/cosmicMath/domainInvariants.test.ts`](../../src/utils/cosmicMath/domainInvariants.test.ts) asserting independent physical laws:
* **Vis-Viva Specific Orbital Energy**:
  $$\mathcal{E}_{\text{norm}} = \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{v}{v_0}\right)^2 - \frac{1}{r/a} = -0.500 \quad (\text{conserved within } < 0.1\% \text{ across all 365 days})$$
* **Keplerian Apsidal Angular Momentum**:
  $$r_{\text{peri}} \cdot v_{\text{peri}} = r_{\text{aph}} \cdot v_{\text{aph}} \quad (\text{conserved within } < 0.015\%)$$
* **Continuous Polar Latitude Sweep**: Evaluated 181 integer latitudes $\phi \in [-90^\circ, +90^\circ]$ across all 4 seasonal milestones with zero `NaN` or unhandled exceptions.
* **Collinear Antipodal Unit Vectors**: $\hat{\mathbf{r}}_\oplus \cdot \hat{\mathbf{s}}_\odot = -1.0000000000$ ($|\Delta| < 10^{-12}$) across all 6 seasonal orbital milestones.

## Consequences

### Positive
* **Mathematical Determinism**: Ephemeris calculations produce finite, well-bounded results across all possible geographic locations ($\pm 90^\circ$), historical epochs (1900–2050), and edge-case inputs.
* **Zero Visual Jitter / NaN Paths**: SVG path strings and canvas coordinates are permanently protected against `NaN` injection.
* **Verifiable Physics**: Physical conservation laws are now an enforced part of the automated regression suite (412 tests across 21 suites).

### Architecture Invariants for Future Contributors
1. **Never Call Bare `Math.asin` or `Math.acos`**: All inverse trigonometric calls must pass their arguments through `clamp(val, -1, 1)`.
2. **Never Divide by Distance or Trigonometric Difference Without an Epsilon Floor**: Use `Math.max(1e-6, divisor)`.
3. **Always Wrap Circular Angles with Canonical Positive Modulo**: Use `(((angle % 360) + 360) % 360)`.
4. **Never Check Numerical Coordinates with Falsy `if (val)`**: Always check `val !== undefined` to prevent zero-angle chirality bugs.
